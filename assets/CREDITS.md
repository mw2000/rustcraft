# Credits

Everything in `assets/` is made for rustcraft and ships under the project's licence (MIT or
Apache-2.0), as the design's INV-8 asks, but for the font below. Nothing from Minecraft or its
resource packs, which are inspiration only (D-10).

- **Textures** (`textures/`): drawn by the block-textures skill's scripts, or generated by it with
  Stable Diffusion 1.5 through ComfyUI (`.claude/skills/block-textures/`).
- **Sounds** (`sounds/`): every footstep, break, splash, creature call, the cave's loop and every
  piece of music is synthesised by the sounds skill's `synth.py` (`.claude/skills/sounds/`), from
  noise, filters, struck modes, bubbles, chirps and plucked strings, and the wind is synthesised
  as it plays (`src/client/sound/ambience/wind.rs`). The other ambience loops and the owl are
  recordings, below.
- **Structures** (`structures/`): built in the game or written by the structures skill.

- **Font** (`fonts/FiraMono-Regular.ttf`): Fira Mono, by The Mozilla Corporation and Telefonica
  S.A., under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/FiraMono-OFL.txt`), from
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/firamono. It's the whole of the font Bevy embeds
  a subset of, with the symbols the subset lacks, such as ° and ×, and it's built into the game.

A file from elsewhere, such as one of Kenney's CC0 packs, gets a line here with its pack, where it
came from and its licence.

## Recordings

Chosen and prepared by Aymen Qader for upstream rustcraft's ambience (rornic/rustcraft #77), and
ported from there. Every one is CC0 1.0 (public domain), so none needs crediting, but thanks to:

| File | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sounds/ambience/birds.ogg` | [Birds in the Slovenian Forest - Processed w/ Generated Noise](https://freesound.org/people/GammaGool/sounds/850507/) (Freesound) | GammaGool | CC0 1.0 |
| `sounds/ambience/crickets.ogg` | [Crickets At Night - Raw sound](https://freesound.org/people/Defelozedd94/sounds/522299/) (Freesound) | Defelozedd94 | CC0 1.0 |
| `sounds/ambience/sea.ogg` | [Sea Waves](https://bigsoundbank.com/sea-waves_s0698.html) (BigSoundBank) | Joseph Sardin | CC0 1.0 |
| `sounds/ambience/underwater.ogg` | [Underwater Ambience.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Tim_Verberne/sounds/482167/) (Freesound) | Tim_Verberne | CC0 1.0 |
| `sounds/ambience/desert.ogg` | [Looping Gentle Wind Ambience on an Open Desert Plain](https://freesound.org/people/dhallcomposer/sounds/697217/) (Freesound) | dhallcomposer | CC0 1.0 |
| `sounds/ambience/leaves.ogg` | [Bushes Rustling.WAV](https://freesound.org/people/G.Lamont/sounds/117884/) (Freesound) | G.Lamont | CC0 1.0 |
| `sounds/owl.ogg` | [Tawny owl hooting](https://freesound.org/people/Patrick_Corra/sounds/745208/) (Freesound) | Patrick Corrà | CC0 1.0 |

Each loop is a 30 s window of its recording (24 s for the leaves), crossfaded into a seamless
loop. The crickets are high-passed at 2.5 kHz to take out the storm behind them, the owl is one
hoot with the recording's background noise gated out, and the leaves are a close recording of
bushes with the rumble cut, the top softened and a short reverb spread over it, so it sounds like
a canopy overhead. Here each is then levelled once to the sounds skill's targets (loops -27 dB,
the owl -21) by its `recordings.py`.
