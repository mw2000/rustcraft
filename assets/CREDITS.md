# Credits

Everything in `assets/` is made for rustcraft and ships under the project's licence (MIT or
Apache-2.0), as the design's INV-8 asks. Nothing here comes from anyone else, and nothing from
Minecraft or its resource packs, which are inspiration only (D-10).

- **Textures** (`textures/`): drawn by the block-textures skill's scripts, or generated by it with
  Stable Diffusion 1.5 through ComfyUI (`.claude/skills/block-textures/`).
- **Sounds** (`sounds/`): every footstep, break, splash, creature call, ambience loop and piece of
  music is synthesised by the sounds skill's `synth.py` (`.claude/skills/sounds/`), from noise,
  filters, struck modes, bubbles, chirps and plucked strings. No recordings or sample packs.
- **Structures** (`structures/`): built in the game or written by the structures skill.

A file from elsewhere, such as one of Kenney's CC0 packs, gets a line here with its pack, where it
came from and its licence.
